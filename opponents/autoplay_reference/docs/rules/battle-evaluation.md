# Greedy battle evaluation rules

This section decides which of our legal moves, Mega variants or switches is best. Policy `greedy-showdown-v2` uses a probability-gated setup/priority policy over Showdown outcomes. The scoring formula stays unchanged.

All rules below are implemented. This file describes current behavior; Markdown is documentation, and changing it alone does not change the bot.

## Current rules

| ID | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| BE-01 | Consider established tablet actions: usable move slots and eligible living switches, filtered by mandatory Mega Evolution under BE-21. Intersect them with regenerated Showdown legality. Fresh verified PP overrides stale caption counters. Normal Choice-item turns reuse the observed last move or corroborated lock, retaining the locked move and every eligible switch for the existing scoring policy. Explicit Choice restriction OCR is a state-repair fallback that triggers rescoring within the original budget. A matching accepted native action plus reduced PP can establish its Choice lock within the same stint, without inventing battle effects. Rejected warnings are not accepted execution and do not advance the prompt/turn. Unknown PP, unsupported copied move slots, locks, trapping and other restrictions can exclude an action; a known move name alone does not establish availability. |
| BE-02 | Rebuild the simulator root from observations plus explicit assumptions. Clone it independently for each candidate/seed. Hypothetical states never update live observations, and hydration must not retrigger entry effects or advance a turn. |
| BE-03 | Generate and save 32 random seeds. Evaluate every candidate against the same fixed opponent reply and seeds. Complete every candidate and pending prompt in a batch before retaining it. All 32 batches are required for a strategy decision; partial samples remain diagnostic evidence and never select an action. Conditional next-turn groups retain these same 32 trial positions and record which positions apply; they never resample or reseed. |
| BE-04 | Score a nonterminal outcome as `100 * (opponent knockouts - own knockouts) + hp_change`. `hp_change` is our team's summed HP-percentage-point gain minus the opponent team's summed HP-percentage-point gain, relative to the root. Count all three selected members on each side, including bench changes, healing, recoil, hazards and residuals. Count only newly fainted members as knockouts. |
| BE-05 | A terminal win scores +10,000 and a terminal loss -10,000, replacing the ordinary score. A terminal draw has no separate bonus and uses the ordinary HP/KO score. |
| BE-06 | Apply BE-15, then the eligible Palafin exit preference under BE-19, then qualifying setup under BE-13/14, then BE-16. Within each qualifying cohort, maximize mean HP/KO score. Setup scores include both simulated exchanges. Palafin exits and ordinary staying/switching candidates use the same current-plus-next-turn horizon under BE-17/18/19, scored from the original position. Only in the ordinary comparison, when every retained candidate's complete mean score is -10,000, choose the highest mean actual first-turn move-inflicted opponent HP damage before applying stable action order. Use the same 32 trials and applicable conditional indices; misses, immunity and fainting before attacking contribute zero. Future damage cannot replace first-turn evidence. Record the applied tie-break and keep BE-05's terminal scores. Otherwise, and when damage also ties, exact ties retain move-slot order, then switch-row order after mandatory Mega filtering. The earlier preferences and ordinary attack shortlist are unchanged; see the deferred roadmap. |
| BE-07 | Add no arbitrary points for setup, status, hazards, screens or resources. Setup can earn preference only through BE-13/14; its score measures actual two-turn effects. Other Status moves remain options under immediate HP/KO scoring. |
| BE-08 | At a normal faint replacement, simulate entry separately for each of the 32 trials, then evaluate the next action using the shared entry-continuation planner and this same policy, including bounded setup lookahead. Continue each state/PRNG. Do not recursively extend switches inside that continuation. At a mid-turn replacement, resolve remaining current-turn actions; observed Flip Turn, U-turn and Volt Switch prompts additionally include exactly the next turn under BE-20. Other or unknown mid-turn causes retain their existing horizon. Hypothetical replacement chains use TS-09 except explicit BE-20 pivot receivers, never recursive policy search. |
| BE-09 | Use the inclusive turn/send/preview ceilings and execution reserves defined only in the [central timing settings](../../bot/runtime/budget.py), accounting for screen preparation within the configured phase budget. OCR move/game/preview countdowns are not read and never cap deadlines. Live decisions and helpers read these settings at call time; offline replay defaults to its saved phase, with an optional explicit per-run override. The Node worker requires the supplied absolute deadline and has no independent timeout default. If a required comparison fails or cannot finish all 32 trials, record the incomplete stage and use BE-23. Reserve the final 20% of the decision allowance for BE-23, without enlarging the inclusive ceiling. Never extend the deadline or select from completed early candidates. |
| BE-10 | Retired on 2026-10-27. Historical first-slot/first-row selection remains inspect-only. BE-23 defines scored recovery and BE-24 defines the separate explicit tactical policy. |
| BE-11 | Save observed inputs, roster identity, predictions, legal/rejected actions, clocks, assumptions, exact root, simulator/data identities, seeds, reply, candidate outcomes/score components and selected action. Runtime records executed action, confirmation/retries/failures and timing. Preserve offline replay evidence. |
| BE-12 | Ordinary immediate-KO and priority thresholds remain strictly greater than 70%: 22/32 fails and 23/32 passes. Setup uses separate inclusive thresholds: 100% survival and at least 85% boosted KOs across the original 32 trials (32 survivors and at least 28 KOs). Every comparison requires all 32 positions without resampling. Conditional next-turn groups retain applicable indices/counts; inactive positions cannot inflate a KO rate or receive an invented exchange. |
| BE-13 | Gate Status moves that can boost our Attack/Sp. Atk. The stat used by the follow-up must be below +3 before setup: +2 Swords Dance may reach +4; Belly Drum may reach +6. Mixed boosters check the relevant offensive stat separately. Conditional self boosts include non-Ghost Curse, Growth, Howl and Acupressure; enemy boosts are excluded. Actual Showdown effects/costs determine success. |
| BE-14 | Require survival after the entire setup exchange in all 32 trials, including HP costs and residuals. Require at least 85% boosted KOs across all original trials by one fixed follow-up attack, so 27/32 fails and 28/32 passes. The attack must use an offensive stat actually increased by setup. Unavailable actions, lost/ignored boosts, another Pokemon's offensive stat or forced departure cannot count. Recompute each post-setup reply, fixed across that state's attacks; continue the exact serialized state/PRNG with no recursive boosting. Re-evaluate from OCR on the next real turn. |
| BE-15 | An attack with >70% immediate move-caused KO probability precedes setup. Prefer a priority attack with >70% KOs before the predicted enemy action when an ordinary attack would act later. Use Showdown's resolved priority/order/Speed, including field and ability effects. Sucker Punch/Fake Out conditions and priority blocking are simulated. Compare qualifying actions by mean HP/KO score. Recoil/contact/residual damage still count. |
| BE-16 | Outside BE-19, shortlist the non-pivot damaging attack with highest mean actual first-turn move-inflicted opponent HP damage, and retain every established legal Flip Turn, U-turn and Volt Switch under BE-20. Also retain every established legal damaging move whose pinned catalog flags include charge, regardless of first-turn damage. Preserve original action order and include an action only once when it also wins the damage shortlist. Charging attacks use the existing bounded continuation and HP/KO score, including boost/lock/weather/item mechanics and failed strikes; add no setup bonus, new threshold or extra turn. Misses, immunity and fainting before attacking contribute zero. Break non-pivot damage ties by first-turn HP/KO score then stable action order. Freeze this shortlist while comparing attacks, pivots, switches and other eligible Status moves by their complete bounded scores. Setup that failed BE-13/14 is excluded. Pivoting has no general preference over staying in. Next-turn damage/KOs must not replace first-turn facts or create an immediate-KO preference. |
| BE-17 | Before the ordinary comparison, extend each voluntary switch through exactly the next turn using the shared faint-replacement continuation planner. First simulate switching, entry hazards, the fixed predicted enemy action and residuals. Continue all 32 exact resulting roots/PRNGs without rehydration or reseeding; predict a fresh reply for each resulting state, assuming no voluntary enemy switch. Score from the original pre-switch state through the next exchange, retaining all entry damage, healing, knockouts and subsequent effects. No recursive switching or extra setup-to-attack turn is added inside this paired comparison; offensive setup cannot qualify beyond its common horizon. Actual normal faint-replacement decisions retain BE-08's setup policy. BE-19 reuses this planner at its earlier Palafin exit-preference stage; outside BE-19, immediate-KO and qualifying-setup preferences remain ahead of the ordinary comparison. Any required continuation failure/incompleteness uses BE-23, never the first-turn switch score. Save entry rows, replies, continuation options/outcomes, chosen plans and aggregate scores. |
| BE-18 | Extend the ordinary shortlisted staying-in attacks, including charging attacks retained by BE-16, and other non-offensive-setup Status moves over the same current-plus-next-turn horizon as voluntary switches. Use a legal current move, never a fabricated pass or guaranteed death. Let Showdown determine survival/faints in each of the same 32 trials, resolving hypothetical replacements through TS-09. Continue the actual surviving active or replacement into the next exchange; an opponent attack already spent on the fainted member is not applied again. Hazards, residuals and next-turn attacks still apply. Group next-turn plans by the observed active identity, choosing one common legal follow-up across that identity's applicable trials before using its future outcomes; never pick a different winning move per seed. Reply prediction and normal one-exchange selection are reused, without another setup turn or recursive continuation. Score every final state once from the original pre-decision position under BE-04/05, including the sacrificed member's remaining HP and knockout, its damage before fainting, replacement damage and all enemy losses. Terminal trials end normally. Require every candidate/conditional group to complete its recorded 32-position work before choosing any action; otherwise use BE-23. |
| BE-19 | Only at our move prompt with a living current Zero-form Palafin, an established legal Flip Turn and a living teammate, prefer a working exit after BE-15 and before setup or ordinary attack/sacrifice ranking. Use its actual current species, never the potential Hero matchup form or identity name alone; mixed Zero/Hero conditional groups are ineligible. Compare Flip Turn regardless of damage rank with every legal voluntary switch. BE-20 evaluates each receiving teammate separately. Filter receiver paths before maximizing their mean: a working exit leaves Palafin alive in Hero form after the first exchange in at least one applicable trial. Exclude zero-success exit paths while retaining all failed trials in each mean. There is no 50% cutoff, extra survival threshold or artificial Hero bonus. Compare working exits through exactly the next turn using BE-17/18/20 with the same 32 positions and original HP/KO scoring. If no exit works, resume BE-13/14/16/18. This rule belongs to Pokemon rules; Flip Turn mechanics/receiver planning belong to the shared move rule. Existing KO evidence, completeness, terminal scores, bounded horizons and stable ties remain. |
| BE-20 | For established legal Flip Turn, U-turn and Volt Switch, retain the move alongside the strongest non-pivot attack, switches and eligible Status moves in ordinary comparison. Save each genuine own self-switch prompt, then branch each legal receiving identity from that exact serialized root/PRNG. Choose one fixed receiver across all applicable positions of the same 32 trials, by the highest complete mean HP/KO score; never average per-trial best receivers. Ordinary and Palafin exit comparisons include the remaining first turn plus exactly the next turn, scored once from the original position. Inside bounded continuations use only the remaining exchange. Earlier KO/setup gates retain their existing horizons; all receiver entries still complete, and actual recognized pivot prompts receive their next-turn comparison. Failed or blocked pivots do not invent switches. Faints, contact damage, hazards, residuals, healing, immunity, priority and field order use actual mechanics; a spent enemy attack is not repeated. Explicit receivers override TS-09 at the matched pivot only; subsequent faint/hazard chains retain TS-09. A receiver unavailable in a trial retains that trial's recorded automatic outcome and rejection count. Identical automatic receiver results may be reused without changing state or randomness. At an observed live pivot prompt from these three moves, rerank available replacements from the observed state through the remaining current turn and next turn, excluding already-dealt pivot damage. Unknown/other pivot causes retain their current behavior. Keep move/send action IDs, original party-row receiver ties, terminal handling, strict 32-trial completeness and existing deadline/fallback behavior. Record every receiver path, entry/outcome, success/rejection count, selected receiver, applicable indices, horizon and source identity. |
| BE-21 | When an established legal Mega move exists, retain it and remove the non-Mega version of that move. Apply the shared Python filter to live actions, continuations, contextual probes, opponent options and BE-23 scored recovery. Switching never triggers evolution. Once the side has evolved, later stone holders remain base; an already evolved member keeps its form. Multiple held stones remain legal. Only observations change live Mega-used flags; hypothetical evolutions stay in their roots. |
| BE-22 | An unread or unknown View Log entry retains the affected field's last confirmed value; record possible staleness and continue normal scoring. Do not infer missing boosts from a move or PP expenditure. Other valid observations update normally. Scope uncertainties to their owner and field stint; departures, faints and confirmed resets retire obsolete entries. A turn that fails to apply and a page still unreadable after two reads are treated the same way (scope `log`; runtime/log_state.py, read at every prompt); a Battle Info card with all seven stat stages read is a confirmed reset of that side's boost entries, and its other shown facts are recorded as re-observed. Current identity, HP, item and established action-legality checks remain required. Opposing transformations use the recorded target snapshot without replaying entry effects. |
| BE-23 | If the primary simulation comparison fails and time remains, restart/reverify a failed worker once inside the original deadline and use the reserved allowance for a separate complete one-exchange HP/KO comparison over all established legal damaging moves, other non-offensive-setup moves and switches after BE-21. Reuse the fixed reply policy and original 32 seeds; all candidates must complete all 32 trials. No offensive setup/lookahead or KO/setup/Palafin preference gates apply. Faint replacement compares entry plus one following exchange; mid-turn replacement compares entry and remaining actions without repeating spent actions. Resolve intermediate choices through the shared Python replacement policy. Failed-switch execution rereads identities/HP and calls this same recovery within the original ceiling. If prediction/reconstruction cannot support simulation, or recovery fails/expires, use BE-24. Preview retains the complete statistical selector. Save primary/recovery inputs/outcomes/errors independently. |
| BE-24 | Active battles retain established facts and require fresh UI verification before execution. If an incomplete HUD becomes complete on rereading, resume normal scoring with the original prompt/deadline and count both reads as preparation; do not force tactical recovery from the earlier failure. Unresolved rereads retain unknown identity/HP rather than substitute stale observations. When scoring is unavailable, the shared Python `confirmed-tactical-v1` policy ranks usable damaging moves by power × accuracy × STAB × effectiveness × relevant known offensive stat/stage, respecting known current forms/types. If none has positive utility, rank confirmed living replacements by lowest worst weakness to revealed damaging attack types (otherwise known enemy types), then remaining HP percentage, then row. With no damaging action/replacement, prefer non-setup status moves, then setup, with stable slot ties. Retain BE-21 Mega filtering. Log `heuristic_recovery`, reasons, original errors, prompt identity and all inputs; claim no simulated trials/confidence. Cache unchanged failed prompt/observation fingerprints to avoid rerunning deterministic failed searches; changed facts or a new prompt permit search again. Unknown actions cause observation without blind input or artificial turn advancement. |

For example, taking an opponent from 30% HP to a knockout while our team loses 40 HP percentage points gives a net HP change of `30 - 40 = -10`. With one opponent knockout and none of our own, the score is `100 - 10 = 90`, unless the battle ends with a win or loss.

## Example: sacrifice versus switching now

Implemented under BE-17/18 on 2026-10-26. Both ordinary staying-in moves and voluntary switches include the current turn and exactly the next turn.

Let Showdown determine whether the active actually faints in each trial. After a faint, use the shared hypothetical replacement policy and continuation; if the active survives, continue from that actual trial state. The same 32 seeds, serialized states and existing immediate-KO/setup precedence apply. Follow-ups are fixed per active identity across applicable trials, rather than chosen after inspecting each seed's future.

Score both routes from the original state before any sacrifice, using the unchanged BE-04/05 formula. This retains the active's remaining HP loss and knockout penalty, any damage it deals before fainting, hazards, entry effects and the replacement's next exchange. An entry after the opponent has already spent its attack on the fainted Pokemon avoids that attack; hazards and the following turn's attacks still apply. No separate bonus for a free entry is needed.

Illustration, with other party members remaining: the active has 5% HP, a teammate has 100%, the opponent has 60%, and the faster opponent removes 60 HP percentage points from that teammate per attack. Assume no hazards or other HP effects and that the teammate can KO the opponent if it survives to attack.

| Route | Outcome through the next exchange | Existing HP/KO score |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Switch now, then attack | Teammate loses 60 points on entry, then its last 40 before it can attack next turn. Active survives on the bench. | `-100 own knockout - 100 own HP = -200` |
| Switch now, then switch back | Teammate loses 60 points on entry. On the next turn the weak active switches back in and loses its remaining 5 to the attack. | `-100 own knockout - 65 own HP = -165` |
| Stay, faint, then replace | Active loses 5 points and faints. Teammate enters after that attack, takes 60 next turn, then KOs the opponent's remaining 60. | `100 * (1 enemy KO - 1 own KO) + 60 enemy HP - 65 own HP = -5` |

The comparison prefers sacrificing here because it preserves the teammate long enough to get the KO. It also considers switching back on the following turn when legal. In other positions preserving the active can score better. A terminal win/loss still uses BE-05 instead of the ordinary score.
